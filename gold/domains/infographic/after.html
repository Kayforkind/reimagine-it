What can we count without inventing a number?

Six dated things. One magnitude.

The notebook names three places and three signals, each with a year. Position on a shared 1836–1995 scale is the encoding. The only area it ever gives — Big Bend’s eight hundred thousand acres — is drawn as eight equal units, not a bigger park.
