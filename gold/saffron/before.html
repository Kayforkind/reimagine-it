Saffron & Smoke

A small kitchen in Margate. Six dishes. Open fire, open Thursday through Saturday.

The dishes

  • Smoked aubergine

    Whole aubergine charred over applewood, whipped with tahini, pomegranate seeds, mint oil. Served with wood-fired flatbread. £12.

  • Lamb shoulder

    Twelve-hour slow roast with saffron, preserved lemon, and rose harissa. Falls off the fork. £26 for two.

  • Fire-roasted cauliflower

    Half head, blistered in the coals, drenched in smoked butter and toasted pistachio crumb. £14.

  • Pistachio labneh

    Strained overnight, topped with honey-roast pistachios, za'atar, and a slick of olive oil from the West Bank. £9.

  • Za'atar flatbread

    Wood-fired to order. The dough proofs for 24 hours. Served with house labneh. £7.

  • Saffron rice pudding

    Arborio rice, cardamom, saffron threads, burnt honey. Served cold in a clay pot. £8.
