Alamo · San Antonio · 1836

Alamo

A small stone mission at the center of the Texas Republic. Nineteen days, one long siege, one story that carried a state — a limestone church that outlived every regime that ever tried to hold it.

1836
Republic
02
Big Bend · west Texas · 1944

Big Bend

Eight hundred thousand acres along the Rio Grande. Cottonwood, prickly pear, one of the darkest night skies in North America — a Chihuahuan desert park where the horizon does most of the talking.

800k
acres
03
Austin · on the Colorado · 1839

Austin

Live music, live oaks, live legislature. Named after Stephen F. Austin, the empresario who first brought settlers under Mexican rule — the capital that grew up on the Colorado River in 1839.

1839
Capital

Signals

flag · flower · mammal
1839

Lone Star flag

Blue for loyalty, white for purity, red for bravery. One five-point star. Only US flag that ever flew over a sovereign republic.

1901

Bluebonnet

State flower. Only blooms in spring, only along the highway shoulders and the pastures where the ranchers let it.

1995

Longhorn

State large mammal. Named for the horns, which can reach seven feet from tip to tip. A living remnant of the Spanish criollo cattle.
