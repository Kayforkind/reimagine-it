A Letter to the Night Tide

At the edge of the harbor, the night keeps a different kind of time. The water remembers every light that has crossed it.

A slower story

AAt the edge of the harbor, the night keeps a different kind of time. The water remembers every light that has crossed it.

I came for the lighthouse, but the journey began in the dark between two familiar streets. A small blue lamp made the shore feel briefly possible.

What remains

In the morning, the harbor was ordinary again. That is the gift of the night: it lets a small light become a map without asking it to become a sun. I wrote this letter in 2026, after the long walk home. Keep the lamp on for the next traveler.
