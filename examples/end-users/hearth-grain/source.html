The morning bake gallery

Each day the loaves are photographed before the doors open — a photo collection of crusts, crumb, and flour dust pinned to the gallery wall by the counter. The visual study grows one loaf at a time; this week's sheet shows sixteen.

Getting your bread

Ask at the counter the evening before for a 7 AM slot from the oven door. Whole loaves only on Saturdays — the wood oven rests on Mondays. Standing weekly baskets for cafés on the same block save 10 percent.

Hours

The counter opens 7 AM to 4 PM, Tuesday through Sunday. First Saturday of each month is kids' shaping day at 9 AM — flour guaranteed everywhere.

Wholesale

Cafés, supper clubs, and the farmers market co-op can set up a weekly basket — hello@hearthandgrain.example. Last quarter 320 loaves went to the food bank from the Saturday surplus list.
