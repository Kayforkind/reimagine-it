Architecture

MA living building on the river bend. Meridian Tower rises 218 meters across 48 floors in amber #FFB300 cladding, steel #1B2A3A frames, and copper #D97742 fins, with sky #3BA7FF glass that tracks the light.

The upper 14 floors hold 96 residences with floor-to-ceiling glass and private terraces. Every unit faces the water; the corner suites get both river and skyline views. Sales open June 8, 2026.

Residences

Meridian sits 5 minutes from the central station and 12 minutes from the airport. The riverside promenade connects directly to the lobby at grade.
