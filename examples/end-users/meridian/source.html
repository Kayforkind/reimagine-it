Meridian Tower

A living building on the river bend. Meridian Tower rises 218 meters across 48 floors in amber #FFB300 cladding, steel #1B2A3A frames, and copper #D97742 fins, with sky #3BA7FF glass that tracks the light.

Architecture

  • 48 floors · 218 meters · 12 elevator cores
  • 35,000 square meters of office and retail space
  • 7 sky gardens · 1 rooftop observatory
  • LEED Gold · 40% lower energy use than baseline

Residences

The upper 14 floors hold 96 residences with floor-to-ceiling glass and private terraces. Every unit faces the water; the corner suites get both river and skyline views. Sales open June 8, 2026.

Amenities

A 20-meter lap pool, a winter garden, and a 24-hour concierge run on the 4th floor. Rooftop observatory hours are sunrise to 10 PM daily, and the sky bar hosts 2 events a week.

Location

Meridian sits 5 minutes from the central station and 12 minutes from the airport. The riverside promenade connects directly to the lobby at grade.
