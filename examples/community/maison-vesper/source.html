Maison Vesper — Autumn/Winter 2026 Campaign

The Autumn/Winter 2026 campaign, “Concrete Bloom”, was photographed by Ines Vallier across three districts of Marseille over five days in February 2026 — 42 final frames from 1,900 shots, styled entirely in tailored wool, oxidized silk, and raw canvas.

The looks

  • Look 01 — Le Manteau Carrier, double-breasted wool greatcoat
  • Look 02 — La Robe Oxidized, hand-dyed silk column dress
  • Look 03 — Le Canvas Ordinaire, unlined workwear jacket
  • Look 04 — La Veste Sable, sand-washed leather biker
  • Look 05 — Le Tailleur Blanc, bone-white two-piece suit
  • Look 06 — La Chemise Nuit, midnight poplin shirt dress

Campaign facts

Photography: Ines Vallier. Styling: Tomas Reyes. 12 pages in the print edition, out September 18, 2026. The film premiere screens at the Marseille fashion week on October 2, 2026. Campaign budget: €1.2M across print, film, and out-of-home in 6 cities.

Stockists

The collection arrives in 34 stores worldwide on September 25, 2026. Press and appointments: press@maisonvesper.example or visit maisonvesper.example.
