Crimson Circuit

Three nights, three stages, one loud city. The festival runs on magenta #FF2D78, electric yellow #FFD400, and a deep violet night #12001F — with mint flashes #00E5A0 between sets.

Lineup

  • Main Stage · 40 artists · Friday 9 PM until sunrise
  • Neon Tent · live painting and laser shows · Saturday
  • Bass Garden · after-hours sets · Sunday 2 AM

Tickets

Weekend passes cost 149 dollars and include all three stages. 12,000 passes are on sale until June 20, 2026. Student discounts are 20% with a valid ID.

Getting there

The site sits on the old railway yard, 5 miles from downtown. Shuttles run every 15 minutes from the central station from 4 PM to 6 AM. Camping opens Thursday at noon.

Contact

Press and artist passes at press@crimsoncircuit.fm.
