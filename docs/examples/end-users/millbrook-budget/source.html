Millbrook — 2026 City Budget Report

The proposed budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2026, published for public comment by the Millbrook finance committee. Posters at the library carry the civic navy #1F3A5F header band, brass gold #C9A227 figures, and paper cream #F2EFE6 backgrounds with slate blue #5E718A footnotes.

Where the money goes

  • Road resurfacing and bridges — $4,800,000
  • Water main replacement — $2,300,000
  • Fire and emergency response — $3,100,000
  • Library and community rooms — $640,000
  • Parks and tree planting — $890,000
  • Bus service expansion — $1,150,000

Compare: 2025 versus 2026

Compare the adopted 2025 budget of $11,200,000 with the proposed 2026 budget of $12,700,000 — a 13 percent increase. Per resident, spending rises from $918 to $1,024 across an estimated 12,400 residents. Debt service falls 6 percent.

Timeline of the budget cycle

  • March 3, 2026 — draft published for public comment
  • April 21, 2026 — town hall on the paving and water plan
  • June 30, 2026 — council vote
  • July 1, 2026 — fiscal year begins
  • February 2027 — mid-year review

How the plan was surveyed

The priorities come from a resident survey: 3,050 responses, 61 percent naming road repair first, 22 percent naming the bus expansion. Crossing guards, gutter work, and the fire station roof round out the statistics appendix. Questions go to finance@millbrook.example.
