Marlow Bindery

A small hand bindery in Bristol. Cloth-bound journals, thesis rebinds, and box-making since 2011.

Services

  • Thesis rebind

    Cloth or buckram, foil-stamped spine title, sewn on tapes. Usual turnaround eight to ten days. From forty pounds.

  • Journal in a run of one

    A5 or A6, 96 or 192 pages, endpaper of your choice, hand-stitched, one at a time. From twenty-eight pounds.

  • Clamshell box

    For a book, a bundle of letters, or a small archive. Cut and covered by hand, felt-lined optional. Priced by size.
